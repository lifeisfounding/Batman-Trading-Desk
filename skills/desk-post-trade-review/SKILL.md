# Post-trade review

## Journal

`/workspace/trading-desk/journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — append-only. Proposals opened, PASS/REJECT, approvals, sends, fills, cancels, limit changes, one-line lesson.

## Trade review

Reconstruct from proposal file + MCP order/position/realized pnl tools.

Grade **process** and **outcome** separately. One repeatable learning.

## Weekly review

Trade count, R distribution, costs as share of gross, max drawdown, incidents, process-break patterns. No strategy advice — hand that to Blake with numbers.

## Incident review

Timeline, what controls did, one corrective action with owner. Blameless tone.
