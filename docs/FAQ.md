# FAQ

**What do I actually get?**
Seven Bots in your Grok Bot workspace, one Batcave group chat, shared skills, and a written way of working. Ask for a market brief, a sized trade, a backtest or a review, and the right Bot answers with live data and sources. No house strategy is included.

**What do I need to start?**
Grok Bot, Robinhood Trading MCP connected, and the paste-in prompt from the README. The desk starts in research mode until you set limits and enable live sends.

**How does the desk trade?**
Through Robinhood Trading MCP on the Agentic account only. Robin calls write tools after Lucius PASS and your approval (chat by ticket id or a scoped standing approval).

**How do I approve a trade?**
Lucius posts a ticket with an id like `BT-20260921-01`. You type `approve BT-20260921-01`. That exact phrase, after seeing that exact ticket, lets Robin send once (within the ticket expiry). Standing approvals, if any, must be written into `desk.md` / `risk-limits.md` with date and scope.

**Where do the ideas come from?**
From you. Blake turns your idea into rules and tests it honestly; Oracle and Vicki give you the picture; you decide.

**Why six on the floor and one off it?**
Grok Bot group chats hold six Bots, and reviews are calmer after the noise. Gordon works by DM.

**Can I rename the Bots?**
You can change avatars; keep the **Name (Role)** display form if you want SETUP and skills to stay consistent. Keep one writer: only Robin has broker write skills.

**Can it run while I sleep?**
Routines can brief you and alert. Sending waits for approval unless you deliberately wrote a standing approval — and even then Auto-review may ask for a tap.

**What if a send times out?**
Robin reconciles by order id / ref_id, tells you whether the order exists, and asks for a fresh approval only if it does not.

**Is this financial advice?**
No. It is documentation and instructions.
